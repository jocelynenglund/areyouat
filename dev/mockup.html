Design language

  • Warm sand background (#f5ede0), orange accent (#ff6b35) — feels like a Swedish summer evening
  • One action per screen. The button is always obvious.
  • Swinglish copy: "ingen e här", "men jag är!", "vem e du?" — casual, friend-group voice
  • Passphrase = free text, not PIN. More memorable, harder to guess.
  • Presence items show first name + time only. No avatars, no last names.
  • Everything resets at midnight — yesterday's presence never shows today.
  • Mobile-first. Will scale to desktop by centering the phone-width column.
